Modern Architecture

Modern architecture is an architectural style based on new and innovative technologies of construction, particularly the use of glass, steel, and reinforced concrete. It emerged in the first half of the 20th century and became dominant after World War II. The style is characterized by minimalism, open floor plans, and a focus on functionality over ornamentation.

Ancient Architecture

Ancient architecture is characterized by its grandeur and use of durable materials like stone and marble. From the pyramids of Egypt to the temples of Greece and Rome, ancient builders created structures that have stood the test of time. These buildings often featured columns, arches, and intricate carvings that reflected the cultural and religious values of their societies.
